## 1-2. LT-I Domestic slabs, energy charges, fixed and customer charges

How the slab group is decided (§4.2.3, p. 247): by that month's consumption, not an annual average.
- "The sub-category LT-I(A) shall be applicable to the consumers having consumption not exceeding 100 units per month."
- "LT-I(B)(i) shall be applicable to the consumer with consumption above 100 units but not exceeding 200 units per month."
- "LT-I(B)(ii) shall be applicable to the consumer having consumption of above 200 units per month"

Fixed charge: the table header is "Fixed Charge (Rs. /month)" with Unit "kW" (§4.2.4, p. 247). The order's LT-I section does not say which kW the fixed charge applies to (contracted or connected load): **NOT FOUND**.
Customer charge: from the "Customer Charges" table, "LT-I: Domestic (Units/month)", p. 266.
The order does not say in words that the slabs are telescopic (each slab's rate applies only to the units inside it): **NOT STATED**. The table layout (0-50, 51-100 ...) suggests it but this is inferred.

Notes:
- The customer-charge table (p. 266) is keyed by the household's total monthly units (0-50, 51-100, 101-200, 201-300, 301-400, 401-800, above 800), not by slab group. So a 150-unit home pays Rs 90, a 250-unit home Rs 100. That reading is from the table's header "LT-I: Domestic (Units/month)"; the order does not spell out the rule further.
- Other LT-I charges seen: delayed payment surcharge "LT-I(A) Rs.10 per month" and "LT-I(B), ... Rs.25 per month" (p. 257). Capacitor surcharge does not apply to domestic: "In the case of LT consumers (except LT Domestic, ...)" (§4.15.3, p. 267).

## 3. Net-metered rooftop solar consumers

- Billing on net units: **yes**. TGDISCOMs' reply (§3.30.2, p. 230): "Under the net metering framework, the net units (import minus export) are considered for billing, while the applicable tariff slab is determined based on the total import units. In cases where export exceeds import, the excess units are settled at the applicable rate and adjusted in subsequent bills." (This is the DISCOMs' statement, not the Commission's ruling. The "applicable rate" for excess export is not given in this order: **NOT FOUND**.)
- Special rule in this order: the slab must also be decided on net units. Commission (§3.30.4, p. 230): "the Commission directs TGDISCOMs to revert to the earlier practice of determining tariff slabs based on net consumption (import minus export) for net metered solar rooftop consumers. TGDISCOMs are further directed to adjust the excess amounts collected" (continues p. 231: "from such consumers due to the change in billing methodology, against the next three billing cycles from the date of issue of this Order.")
- Background (§3.30.1, p. 230): "from October 2025 the TGDISCOMs has changed the billing methodology without prior notice and is applying higher tariff slabs based on gross import units".
- Repeated as Directive 22 in Appendix B (p. 301), same wording, "(Para 3.30.4)".
- The governing regulation is named as the "TGERC Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems Regulation, 2025" (p. 230). Its own text was not opened for R9.

For EcoProof: a net-metered home's bill = slab rates applied to (import − export) for the month, with the slab group also picked from (import − export).

## 4. Domestic subsidy / free units (Gruha Jyothi)

- **Gruha Jyothi: NOT FOUND in this order.** I searched the full text for "Gruha", "Griha", "Jyothi", "free power", "free electricity", "zero bill" and "up to 200": no mention of a free-units scheme for households. The only "free supply" mentioned is for agriculture (§3.2.14, p. 51: "under the policy of free supply"). Gruha Jyothi's terms (who qualifies, the 200-unit limit, how the state pays the DISCOM) need a Government of Telangana source, not this order.
- What the order does have is the state's general tariff subsidy for LT-I Domestic, paid by the Government of Telangana to the DISCOMs under Section 65:
  - Govt letter quoted (§3.28.4, p. 175): "the Government of Telangana has provided a budgetary allocation of Rs.14,000 crores ... under the head of Power Subsidy in the State Budget for FY 2026-27. The Government hereby commits to extending this subsidy support to TGDISCOMs under Section 65 of the Electricity Act, 2003".
  - Table 3-50 (p. 175), "Allocation of subsidy commitment (Rs. Crores) of GoTG for FY 2026-27": "LT-I: Domestic 923.44 868.18 27.16 1,818.79" (TGSPDCL, TGNPDCL, CESS, Total).
  - The unfunded rest goes to a regulatory asset, Table 3-51 (p. 175): "LT-I: Domestic 75.06 70.56 0.00 145.62".
  - Without that subsidy the Commission says DISCOMs would charge full-cost tariffs (§3.27.9, p. 174): "In the absence of any external subsidy u/s 65 of the Act, TGDISCOMs will have to charge the rates contained in the above FCRTS".
