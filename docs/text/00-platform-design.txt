Platform design: features, feasibility, legal and cost
 · 
Bottom line
The platform is buildable for the ideathon with no government approvals, about ₹0–1,000 in cash, and roughly 5–6 working days for a team of four.
Feasibility: all 17 build-now features use public data and standard services. The one real risk is reading poor bill photos; the confirm screen and manual entry cover it.

Approvals: none for the prototype. A real launch needs data-protection compliance by May 2027 and a written agreement with any lender that pays referral fees.

Wording: say "energy and carbon pre-assessment", not "energy audit"; say "loan-ready summary", not "loan approval"; label buyer emissions sheets as estimates.

Cost: the prototype runs on free tiers; a 3-month pilot with 100 MSMEs and 500 households costs about ₹17,000, mostly hosting.

Model choice: Gemini 2.5 Flash reads images, has a free tier of about 1,500 requests a day, and costs $0.30 input and $2.50 output per million tokens on paid use (RapidDev, July 2026). Free-tier prompts may be used by Google for training (Pasquale Pillitteri), so use sample bills, not real customer bills, on the free tier.
Architecture
One backend serves both the web app and WhatsApp, so every bill goes through the same reader, engine and data store.
The reference tables are the trust layer: each emission factor, scheme rule and action carries its source and date, and every result stores which version it used. The stack (React, Express, Prisma, Postgres) is one your team can build quickly.
Legal and regulatory check
The ideathon prototype needs no government approval or licence; a real launch needs data-protection compliance and written agreements with lenders, and two words in the product must be avoided. This is a research summary, not legal advice; check with a lawyer before launch.
Area
 | Applies to
 | What the rule says
 | Ideathon
 | Real launch
 | 
Data protection: DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025
 | All user data; bills carry names and addresses
 | Rules notified Nov 2025; consent-manager registration from Nov 2026; main consent, breach and other duties from 14 May 2027 (Anantam IAS). Notice must be standalone and clear (Mondaq); data deleted when consent is withdrawn; breaches reported within 72 hours (Hogan Lovells)
 | No approval; show the consent screen anyway
 | Comply by May 2027; no registration needed unless we become a consent manager
 | 
Lending: RBI Digital Lending Directions 2025
 | Loan matcher, referrals
 | An agent doing a lender's digital customer acquisition is a Lending Service Provider (RBI) and must work under a written contract with that lender (Vinod Kothari)
 | No issue: the owner downloads the summary and applies directly
 | Any paid referral needs a contract with each lender; never show "approval" or charge borrowers
 | 
GST data
 | Purchase invoices
 | Only GST Suvidha Providers connect to the GST system; apps reach data through a GSP with the taxpayer's OTP consent (IRIS GST)
 | Use invoice photos and PDFs; no approval
 | Partner with a GSP for API import
 | 
Energy Conservation Act 2001
 | Wording of reports
 | "Energy audit" is a defined term, and designated consumers must use accredited energy auditors (BEE)
 | Call our output an "energy and carbon pre-assessment", never an energy audit
 | Same; refer to BEE-certified auditors for real audits
 | 
CBAM
 | Supplier emissions sheet
 | Definitive-phase data needs verification, with on-site visits by verifiers in 2026 (The Sustainability Cloud)
 | Label the sheet "estimate, not verified"
 | Same; route to accredited verifiers
 | 
Carbon credits (CCTS)
 | Future bundling
 | Offset projects register with BEE and need accredited verification (Vision IAS)
 | Not built; don't claim credits
 | Only with BEE registration
 | 
WhatsApp
 | Upload and reminders
 | Per-message charges for templates; Indian accounts must move to INR billing by 31 Dec 2026 (Monty Mobile)
 | Meta test number
 | Business verification and user opt-in before messaging
 | 
Udyam verification
 | Onboarding
 | Verification is offered by private API vendors such as Cashfree
 | Typed number, not verified
 | Commercial agreement with a vendor
 | 
Government names and logos
 | Branding, reports
 | Using official emblems or scheme logos can imply endorsement
 | Mention schemes by name only; no logos
 | Same
 | 
Two words to avoid on screens and in the pitch: "energy audit" (use pre-assessment) and "loan approval" (use loan-ready summary).

The ideathon prototype costs ₹0 to about ₹1,000 in cash and about 22 person-days of work; a 3-month pilot with 100 MSMEs and 500 households costs about ₹17,000. Dollar prices are converted at an assumed ₹88 per US$.
