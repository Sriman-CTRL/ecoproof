  Onboarding and data capture
  The owner should reach a first footprint and a first rupee saving within 10 minutes of
  signing up, using only bill photos.

  ONB-1 Sign-in

   1. Owner enters mobile number; OTP sent by SMS (WhatsApp OTP as alternative).
   2. On success, create user and session; ask for language before anything else.
   3. If the number was invited by another owner, attach the invited role instead of creating
      a new business.

  ONB-2 Business profile

   1. Ask Udyam number (format check only in v1), business name, city, sector (foundry,
      forging, fasteners, other).
   2. Load the sector template: production unit (tonnes of castings, pieces), equipment
      checklist, action library.
   3. Ask DISCOM, consumer number and tariff category (HT-I(A), LT-III); prefilled later from
      the first bill.
   4. Save as an organisation with one site; more sites can be added.

  ONB-3 Language

   1. Telugu, Hindi or English; stored on the user, not the business, so a Telugu owner and an
      English accountant share one account.
   2. All UI text from a translation file; numbers always in Indian format (1,00,000).

  ONB-4 Consent and team

   1. Show a one-screen consent notice: what is stored, why, who can see it, how to delete.
   2. Record consent with timestamp and notice version; nothing is processed before it.
   3. Owner can invite an accountant or supervisor by phone number and pick their role.

  CAP-1 Electricity bill

   1. Owner photographs or uploads a PDF, or forwards it on WhatsApp.
   2. Store the original file; compute a hash to block duplicates.
   3. Send the image to the document reader with a fixed field list: consumer number, billing
      period, kWh, kVAh, maximum demand, contracted demand, PF, ToD units, energy
        charges, demand charges, other line items, total.
   4. Validate: kWh ≤ kVAh, PF between 0.5 and 1.0, period not overlapping a saved bill,
      consumer number matches the site.
   5. Show the confirm screen with the photo beside each value; low-confidence fields
      highlighted.
   6. On confirm, save values tagged "measured", then trigger calculation and the bill health
        check.

  CAP-2 Fuel receipts: same pipeline; fields are fuel type, quantity, unit, date, amount. Unit
  converted to kg or litres from a lookup.

  CAP-3 GST purchase invoices

   1. Extract supplier GSTIN, date, line items, HSN code, quantity, unit.
   2. Map HSN chapters 72 (iron and steel), 73 (articles of iron or steel) and 76 (aluminium)
      to CBAM material categories; ignore other lines.
   3. Store tonnes per material per month for the Scope 3 and CBAM estimate.

  CAP-4 Production entry

   1. On the 1st of each month, a WhatsApp quick-reply asks "How many tonnes did you
        make last month?"
   2. The supervisor or owner replies with a number; it is saved as "owner-entered".
   3. If no reply in 5 days, a reminder; savings then fall back to raw kWh with a warning.

  CAP-5 Backfill: after the first bill, ask for the previous 11 months in one go; a progress bar
  shows "8 of 12 months". The baseline unlocks at 9 months.

  CAP-6 Telugu voice note

   1. Owner records a voice note, e.g. "ee nela 95 tonnulu, diesel 1,200 litres".
   2. Speech-to-text returns Telugu–English text; the language model maps it to fields.
   3. Show the parsed values for one-tap confirm; never save unconfirmed voice values.


  Calculations, insights and actions
  Every confirmed bill triggers one pipeline: calculate, compare, check the bill, refresh the
  action plan.

  CALC-1 Emission engine

   1. Triggered by a confirmed bill, receipt, invoice or production value.
   2. Load the factor row valid for the bill period; compute Scope 1, 2 and metals Scope 3
        (formulas in doc 03).
   3. Save a result row with factor IDs, formula version and input IDs; never overwrite an
        older result.
   4. Recompute monthly and yearly totals and intensity.

  CALC-2 Baseline and benchmark

   1. When 9 or more months exist, fit energy vs production; store coefficients and fit
      statistics.
   2. If the fit fails the thresholds in doc 03, mark the baseline "intensity only".
   3. Compute peer position against cluster peers (10+) or the BEE range.

  INS-1 Bill health check

   1. Run the four checks in doc 03 (power factor, peak use, contracted demand, minimum
        charges) on each new bill.
   2. Convert each finding into ₹ per month and a one-line fix.
   3. Push findings to the top of the action plan as zero-cost actions.

  INS-2 Monthly insight card

   1. After calculation, compare this month with the same month last year and the baseline.
   2. Pick the largest driver (production change, power factor, peak use, fuel) and state it in
      one sentence.

   3. Show ₹ change first, kWh second, CO₂ third.

  ACT-1 Action plan

   1. Filter the sector action library by the owner's equipment checklist.
   2. Estimate each action's saving range, cost range and payback (doc 03).
   3. Rank: zero-cost first, then by payback; show the top 3 with "see all".
   4. Owner marks an action "planned", "in progress" or "done" with date and actual cost.

  ACT-2 Solar sizing

   1. Ask usable roof area and whether the roof is owned.
   2. Compute size from roof, daytime use and the DISCOM net-metering cap; get site yield
      from a solar model.
   3. Show size, yearly generation, ₹ saved, cost range, payback, EMI comparison.

  ACT-3 Vendor quote compare

   1. Owner uploads 1–3 vendor quotations for a planned action.
   2. Extract price, capacity and warranty; flag quotes outside the library's cost range.
   3. The chosen quote feeds the loan-ready pack.

  ACT-4 Assistant

   1. Owner asks a question in any of the three languages, by text or voice.
   2. Retrieve only that business's stored data, plan and the action library entry in question.
   3. The language model answers with a source line ("from your September bill"); questions
        outside the data get "I can only answer about your bills and plan".


  Finance, verification and reports
  This is where the platform differs from every existing calculator: it carries an action to a
  lender and then proves the saving.

  FIN-1 Scheme and loan matcher

   1. Read the business profile (age, GST status, Udyam category, sector, state).
   2. Evaluate each dated rule in the scheme table, e.g. SIDBI green loans need 3+ years of
        operation, cash profits and GST compliance (Mercom).
   3. Show "likely eligible", "check with lender" or "not eligible" with the reason and the rule's
        last-checked date.

  FIN-2 Loan-ready pack

   1. Owner picks a planned action and a vendor quote.
   2. Assemble: business profile, 12-month energy baseline, proposed measure, quote,
        savings range, EMI vs saving, CO₂ impact, data quality score.
   3. Generate a PDF and a read-only link (doc 05).
   4. Never use the words "approved" or "sanctioned"; it is a summary for the owner to
        submit.

  FIN-3 EMI vs saving: for any loan amount, rate and tenure the owner types, show EMI,
  low-end monthly saving and the monthly difference.

  VER-1 Savings verification

   1. After an action is marked done, each new month computes adjusted baseline minus
      actual (doc 03).
   2. Add to the savings ledger in ₹, kWh and CO₂.
   3. Switch the label from "too early to confirm" to "verified" once the significance test
      passes.

  VER-2 Non-routine adjustments: owner logs events that change energy use (new
  machine, extra shift, plant shutdown) with a date and an estimated kWh effect; each
  adjustment appears in reports.

  VER-3 Alerts

   1. Missing bill: no bill 10 days after the expected date.
   2. Spike: actual above adjusted baseline by more than 15% for a month.
   3. New charge: a bill line item not seen before (e.g. a low power factor surcharge).

  REP-1 Monthly report: one page; ₹ saved, kWh per tonne, CO₂ per tonne, top action, data
  quality score.

  REP-2 CBAM supplier sheet: fields mapped to the EU communication template's
  structure (doc 05); every value labelled "estimate, not verified".

  REP-3 BRSR value-chain sheet: energy, emissions and intensity in the BRSR Core format
  for a listed customer.

  REP-4 Progress statement: a yearly PDF with a QR code that opens a verification page
  showing the same numbers, so a buyer or lender can check the document was not altered.

  Engagement, household mode and partner views
  These features keep owners returning every month and bring in new users through
  buyers, lenders and associations.

  ENG-1 Green Progress Score: computed monthly from improvement, data habit, actions
  and peer position (doc 03); shown next to ₹ saved, never alone.

  ENG-2 Leaderboard: cluster and sector only, 10+ units, names hidden unless the owner
  opts in; shows own rank and gap to the unit above (doc 04).

  ENG-3 Monthly WhatsApp summary

   1. When a month's bill is confirmed, send one message: ₹ saved this month, total so far,
      one next action.
   2. Bill-due reminders follow the DISCOM's billing cycle for that consumer number.
   3. All messages are utility templates sent only after opt-in.

  HH-1 Household mode

   1. Simplified sign-up; no Udyam or production questions.
   2. Upload a home electricity bill; compute footprint at the grid factor.
   3. Size rooftop solar, estimate PM Surya Ghar subsidy, link to the official portal.
   4. After installation, read net-metering bills to show imported, exported and ₹ saved.

  PART-1 Lender view

   1. Owner shares a loan-ready pack with a lender's email or phone; link expires in 30 days
      by default.
   2. Lender sees the pack, the data quality score and, later, verified savings for that action.
   3. Every view is logged and visible to the owner.

  PART-2 Buyer data request

   1. A buyer (EU importer or Indian OEM) creates a free account and requests emissions
        data from a supplier by phone or GSTIN.
   2. The supplier gets a WhatsApp invite; if new, they sign up through ONB-1.
   3. Supplier approves the specific report to share; the buyer sees only that report.
   4. Requests and approvals are logged; the owner can revoke at any time.

  PART-3 Cluster association dashboard

   1. Show only aggregates for clusters with 10+ participating units.
